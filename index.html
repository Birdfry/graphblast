How to play

🎯 You vs the bots, turn by turn. On your turn, build a function f(x) with the keypad (or your keyboard) and hit 🔥 Fire.

💥 A shot launches from your tank's center — your tank is the function's origin (0,0): the entire graph of your function across the plane is your weapon, burning outward from your tank in both directions. Aim with offsets from your tank — e.g. enemy 3 right and 2 up? Try 2/3*x. If your function is undefined right at the tank (like ln(x) at x=0), the curve starts at the nearest point where it is defined. Any tank the burning curve touches explodes — one hit kills.

🧱 Walls block the burn — the line stops when it hits one (blasting a crater out of it), so arc over them or thread the gaps.

👀 The dashed line is a live preview of exactly where your shot will go. Tanks show their coordinates underneath to help you aim.

🧱 Blocks litter the board — your line stops when it hits one, blasting a chunk out of it. Arc over them, or smash straight through!

⚠️ The bots shoot back with wobbly aim. Last tank standing wins the round.

👥 Play with friends: hit the 👥 Friends button up top, create a room, and share the 5-letter code — your friend opens the same page and joins. You can even add bots to the mix. ⏱️ In friend matches everyone gets 3 minutes per turn — run out of time and your turn is skipped with a "didn't fire" announcement. The 😈 cheat button is disabled in friend matches.
